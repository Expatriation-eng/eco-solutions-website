THE OPPORTUNITY

A partnership model designed to create real local capacity.

We are approaching established manufacturers, technology companies, suppliers, investors, engineering firms and strategic partners who want more than access to a sales channel. Eco Solutions wants to work with partners prepared to invest alongside us in Lesotho, establish assembly or manufacturing capability, transfer technology and skills, create Basotho jobs, and build a sustainable platform serving Lesotho and regional markets.
