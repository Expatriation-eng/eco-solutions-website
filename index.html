ABOUT ECO SOLUTIONS

Local capability.
Long-term ambition.

Eco Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Basotho-owned and managed company established in 2016 and formally registered under the Business Licensing and Registration Act of 2019 (Registration No. A2022/1224).

The company serves households, businesses, schools, government institutions, financial institutions, hospitals and mining companies across all ten districts of Lesotho.
