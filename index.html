ABOUT ECO SOLUTIONS

Local capability.
Long-term ambition.

Eco Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Basotho-owned and managed company established in 2016 and formally registered under the Business Licensing and Registration Act of 2019.

The company has grown from a construction and technology services provider into a multi-sector enterprise serving households, businesses, schools, government institutions, financial institutions, hospitals and mining companies across all ten districts of Lesotho.
